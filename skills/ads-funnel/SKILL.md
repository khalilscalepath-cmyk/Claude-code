# Ads funnel (AI employee #7, and Scalepath's own lead flow)

From JP Middleton's video. The system: ad → short form or landing page → AI texts the lead within
a minute → keeps going until they book → reminders → review request after the job. Ads alone fail
because nobody follows up fast; this is ads **plus** the follow-up, which is why it's bundled.

## Campaign settings (his, confirm in Ads Manager as Meta changes often)
- Objective: **Leads**. Instant form, or a landing page with the Meta pixel.
- Audience: **broad**. No interests, no custom audiences. Let the creative do the targeting.
  - Scalepath's own ads: United States (he adds Canada and excludes Quebec for language).
  - A client's ads: their service area only (zip codes or a radius around the shop).
- Placements: Advantage+ (automatic). 1 campaign, 1 ad set, 3–5 ads.
- Start at $20–40/day. Judge after about 3–5 days or 50+ leads, not after day 1.
- Kill an ad when cost per lead is 2× the best one. Keep the winner, write 2 new versions of it.
- His partners show leads around $3.60–4 for HVAC and roofing owners. That's his claim from
  screenshots, not a benchmark. Real cost depends on market, offer and form. Track your own.

## Lead quality (cheap leads are often junk)
- Instant form → "Higher intent" type (adds a review step), plus 1–2 qualifying questions:
  - Client ads: "What do you need help with?" (multiple choice) and zip code.
  - Scalepath ads: "How many trucks/techs?" and "Do you own the business?" (yes/no).
- Every lead gets a text within 60 seconds (`client-delivery-agents` speed-to-lead agent).
  Connect Meta lead forms to GoHighLevel (Facebook lead form integration) so it's instant.
- Measure what matters: cost per **booked appointment** and per **showed** appointment, not per lead.

## A. For a client (HVAC / plumbing / electrical)
Offers that work for homeowners: seasonal tune-up price, "$X off a new system", free replacement
estimate / second opinion, water-heater flush, panel safety check. The client approves the offer
and must honor it.

Ad copy (3 versions to test):
1. "{City} homeowners: is your AC 10+ years old? Get a {offer} from {Business}, family-owned since
   {year}. Pick a time in 30 seconds." Button: Get quote.
2. "No AC in July is miserable. {Business} techs are in {city} today. {offer}. Tap to book."
   Button: Book now.
3. Owner-to-camera video (15–30s, phone is fine): who they are, the offer, "tap below and we'll
   text you right away." Real faces beat stock photos.
Images: their real trucks, techs and jobs. Never fake reviews, fake "before/after" or other
companies' photos.

Rules: financing offers ("0% for 60 months") can put the ad in Meta's special category for
financial products, which limits targeting. Use broad targeting anyway, and follow the client's
lender's wording rules. The client owns the ad account and pays Meta directly. You get partner
access, and they never have to ask you for their own account.

## B. For Scalepath (getting your own clients)
Audience: owners of HVAC, plumbing and electrical businesses, and franchise owners. The creative
calls them out by name so Meta finds them.

Ad copy (3 versions to test):
1. "HVAC owners: how many calls hit voicemail while your techs are on jobs? We set up an AI that
   answers, books and texts your tech, 24/7. See a demo built for your business." Button: Get demo.
2. "Plumbers: your old customer list is worth money. We text it, an AI books the jobs, you just
   show up. Free list audit." Button: Learn more.
3. "Own one or more home-service franchise locations? We help franchise owners stop losing
   after-hours calls and old leads. One system, every location." Button: Get demo.
   (Don't put franchise brand names or logos in ads: they're trademarks.)
Then: form → AI texts within 60s → books a 20-minute call on your calendar → `discovery-call`.
Before spending, make sure: a landing page, a demo to show (`voice-receptionist`), a calendar
link, and the speed-to-lead agent running on your own leads.

JP builds his copy with a ChatGPT project trained on his own ad history. The equivalent here is
this skill plus your results: after each test, add the winning ad and its numbers to the bottom of
this file so the next batch starts from what worked.
