# Local business website (Scalepath)

Based on the $500 client-site process (Nafay 3D) plus one scroll moment from ScrollCraft (Nate Herk).
The site's job: **get the phone to ring and the form to fill.** Not to impress designers.

## 1. Interview first (ask, one message, max 5 questions)
1. Business name, trade, city and service area (towns/zip codes).
2. Phone number, hours, and whether they take emergency calls.
3. Top 4–6 services, license number, years in business, brands they install.
4. Real assets: logo, photos of trucks/team/jobs, 3 real reviews with first name + town.
5. Current website URL (scrape it for real copy and details).
If the owner isn't available (cold demo), use only verified public info and mark anything
unconfirmed "to confirm with owner". Never invent reviews, years, licenses or awards.

## 2. References
Pick 2 real, high-converting home-service sites as references (not 3D/WebGL showpieces).
Generate 2–4 concepts; let the user pick one before polishing.

## 3. Page structure (top to bottom)
1. Sticky top bar: logo, tap-to-call number (big), "Book online".
2. Hero: what they do + where, in plain words ("24/7 AC & Heating Repair in Chandler, AZ"),
   one real photo (tech/truck/home), call button + request button, 3 trust facts
   (licensed #, years, rating), "Answered 24/7" badge for the AI receptionist.
3. Services: 4–6 real services with one honest line each.
4. One scroll moment (optional, light): e.g. an after-hours call log that fills in as you scroll.
5. Reviews: real ones only (first name + town). If none provided, show "Google rating X from N
   reviews" with a link, never made-up quotes.
6. Service area: list of towns/zips (and a simple map image if available).
7. Why us: 3 specific facts (licensed, upfront pricing, warranty, same-day) only if true.
8. Request-service form (name, phone, issue, zip) → wired to Speed-to-Lead.
9. Footer: NAP (name, address, phone), hours, license, service area.

## 4. Anti-AI-slop rules (hard)
- No buzzwords: elevate, seamless, unlock, empower, cutting-edge, next-level, game-changer,
  leverage, robust, transform, supercharge, "your comfort is our priority".
- Headlines say what they do and where. Digits, not words ("24/7", "15 years").
- No em-dash habit. No emoji or sparkle icons. No purple/cyan gradients, glow, glassmorphism, blobs.
- No Inter/Roboto/Poppins by default; pick a sturdy display face + readable body face.
- No generic SaaS layout (centered hero + 2 buttons + 3 cards + logo strip + FAQ accordion).
- Real photos of the business beat stock; stock beats AI people; never fake team photos.
- **Acid test:** hide the name and logo. Could it only be this business in this town? If not, fix it.

## 5. Quality checks before showing anyone
Mobile at 390px: call button visible without scrolling; text ≥ 16px; tap targets ≥ 44px;
loads fast (no video backgrounds, compressed images); click-to-call uses `tel:`; form works;
NAP matches Google Business Profile exactly.

## 6. Delivery
Host on the client's domain (e.g. Hostinger via its MCP). Pricing guide: free as the door-opener
when they buy the receptionist; otherwise $500–1,000 build + $30–50/mo hosting and edits.
