# Discovery call (diagnose → quantify → qualify → pitch)

Adapted from Koushik Bethu's recorded close and Mikey's agency playbook.

## Before the call (5 min)
Pull the lead's evidence, website status, hours, review count; have their demo open on speaker.

## Call flow (20–30 min)
1. Open: "What made my email stand out, and what would make this call a win for you?"
2. Diagnose (their words): "Walk me through what happens when a customer calls at 8pm on a
   Saturday." "Who answers when your guys are on jobs?" "What happens to voicemails?"
   "How fast do web/Angi/LSA leads get a call back?" "Do you pay for leads or ads?"
3. Quantify out loud with THEIR numbers: missed calls per week × booking rate (~40%) × average
   ticket × 52. Let them say the yearly number.
4. Qualify: urgency ("is this a now problem or a later problem?"), decision maker, budget range.
   If they don't feel the pain, don't push; ask for a referral.
5. Demo: call their receptionist on speaker; show the booking and the tech SMS.
6. Pitch ONE package that matches what they said (not a menu).
7. Close: price, what's included, 7–10 day launch, **50% upfront / 50% at go-live**, then monthly.

## Pricing guide (by size)
| Size | Package | Setup | Monthly |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–3 techs (< 50 reviews) | Text-Back or Voice AI lite (+ free site if theirs is bad) | $0–500 | $197–397 |
| 4–10 techs (50–300 reviews) | After-Hours Voice AI + Text-Back | $750–1,500 | $497–797 |
| 10–30 techs (300–1,000, runs ads) | Full Lead Recovery | $1,500–3,000 | $997–1,497 |
Optional performance version: lower monthly + $25–50 per booked job.

## Objections
"Customers hate robots" → let them call it; it says it's an AI if asked and books in 2 minutes.
"We already have voicemail" → how many voicemails turn into jobs?
"Too expensive" → compare to one recovered job per month.

## After the call
Write a one-page offer: their problem in their words, the system, what's included, price,
timeline, payment link. Send within 2 hours.
