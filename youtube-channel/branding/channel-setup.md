# Channel setup

## The name

"Science to Earth" is already taken on YouTube. @ScienceToEarth is an existing channel (8 subscribers, 4 science Shorts: goosebumps, hickeys, jumping from space, SpaceX Starship), checked 21 November 2026.

- **If that channel is yours:** keep it and use `banner-science-to-earth.png`.
- **If it isn't yours:** use **Earth to Science**. It's the same idea flipped ("Earth to Science" sounds like "Earth to you, wake up"), short, and the handle @EarthToScience showed no channel when I checked. Use `banner-earth-to-science.png`.

Backups if you want something else: handles @SciencetoEarthTV and @SciencetoEarthHQ also showed no channel. (A handle showing nothing usually means it's free, but YouTube only confirms when you try to claim it.)

## Files

| File | Where it goes in YouTube Studio |
|---|---|
| `profile-800.png` | Customisation → Branding → Picture (800 × 800; YouTube crops it to a circle) |
| `banner-earth-to-science.png` or `banner-science-to-earth.png` | Customisation → Branding → Banner image (2560 × 1440; the text sits inside the area every device shows) |
| `endscreen-1280x720.png` | Not uploaded on its own: add it as the last 15 seconds of each video in your editor, then place YouTube's end-screen elements over the dashed boxes (two videos + subscribe) |

## Channel settings

- Country of residence: yours (affects monetisation eligibility).
- Default upload settings (Settings → Upload defaults): category **Science & Technology**, language **English**, licence **Standard YouTube licence**, comments **on**.
- Audience: **No, not made for kids** (saved as the channel default).
- Turn on **"Test & compare"** for thumbnails on every upload.
