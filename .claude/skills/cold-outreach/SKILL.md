# Cold outreach (Scalepath)

## Structure (every first email)
1. Subject: "Built a small AI demo for {Business}" (or "Quick idea for {Business}").
2. Line 1, personal and true: one specific detail from research (their hours, their 24/7 claim,
   their missing website, their town, their years in business). No flattery.
3. Problem in their words, soft: "When your techs are on jobs, calls that hit voicemail usually
   call the next company on Google." Never paste their reviews back at them.
4. What we built: "an after-hours receptionist that answers like your office, books the job and
   texts your tech" (+ "and a new mobile site" if their site is bad/missing).
5. Proof they can touch: the demo link or "reply 'demo' and I'll send it".
6. Two easy options: Calendly link, or reply for WhatsApp. Low pressure.
Max 120 words. No AI jargon (LLM, agent, automation stack). Digits not words.

## Follow-ups
- Day 3: one line + the demo again ("call it at 9pm like a customer would").
- Day 7: the math: missed calls/week × ~40% booking rate × average ticket × 52.
- Stop after 3 unless they reply.

## Loom script (2 minutes)
0:00 their Google listing / site ("I was looking at {Business}…") → 0:20 the gap (hours, no
answer, no site) → 0:40 call the demo receptionist live, book a job → 1:30 show the tech SMS +
booking → 1:45 "If useful, grab 15 minutes or reply here."

## Compliance
Real name, physical mailing address, working opt-out, honour opt-outs. Warm new domains 2–3 weeks;
≤ 30–50 sends per inbox per day. Send to the owner/manager address when found, not info@ if avoidable.
