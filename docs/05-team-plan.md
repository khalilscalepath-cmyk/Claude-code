# Team Plan: 4–5 Clients, 1–2 Offshore Hires, Khalil as the Face

Goal: Khalil sells and owns client relationships. Offshore help does the repeatable work. Claude
(skills plus cloud routines) does what neither person needs to. All prices are rough 2026 ranges;
check them before relying on them.

## Order (don't hire before revenue)
1. **Clients 1–2: Khalil does everything, with Claude.** You need to know the build well enough to
   check someone else's work, write the SOP, and never be stuck if a hire leaves.
2. **After client 2, or when booking meetings is the bottleneck:** Hire 1, a part-time appointment
   setter / list builder (about 20 h/week). Their daytime is US evening, so after-hours test calls fit
   their shift.
3. **At 4–5 clients:** Hire 2, a part-time GoHighLevel/Vapi builder. Hire for a paid test task (build
   one demo agent from your SOP), not from a CV.

| **Left for Khalil** | **≈ $1,400** | **≈ $3,000** |
Plus setup fees ($1–2k each) as one-off cash. Lesson: at $500/mo two hires eat most of the profit, so
price at $1,000+/mo or reach 8–10 clients before hiring Hire 2.

## Hiring rules
- Where: OnlineJobs.ph (Philippines), Upwork. Paid test task first, then a 2-week trial.
- Access: give each person their own login with limited permissions (GHL user roles, password
  manager). Never share client passwords in chat. Remove access on the same day someone leaves.
- Paper: simple contractor agreement plus NDA; clients' data stays in your accounts.
- Every task has a written SOP (Loom + checklist). Ask Claude to turn each Loom into a skill.
- Measure Hire 1 on meetings booked, and Hire 2 on agents that pass the 6 test calls first time.
