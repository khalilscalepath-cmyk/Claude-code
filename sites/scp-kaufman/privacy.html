Who we are

What we collect

When you call, text, chat or send a request, we collect what you give us: your name, phone number, service address, a description of the problem, and any messages you send. Our phone and chat assistants may keep a transcript or recording of the conversation so we can schedule and complete the job. Like most websites, our host may log basic technical data such as IP address and browser type.

How we use it

We use your information to answer your questions, schedule and complete service, send appointment updates, and improve our service. We do not sell your personal information.

Text messages

If you give us your mobile number, we may text you about your service request and appointment. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time or HELP for help. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be shared with service providers who help us send messages and run our phone, chat and scheduling systems, only so they can provide those services to us.

AI assistants

Our website chat and after-hours phone line use an AI assistant operated by our technology providers. Messages you send are processed by those providers to generate replies. Please don't share payment card numbers or passwords in chat.

How long we keep it

We keep job and contact records for as long as needed to provide service, keep business and warranty records, and meet legal requirements.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by calling (972) 232-2810. Texas residents may have additional rights under Texas law.

Children
