03  Process

One office,
start to finish.

Most builders hand your house between firms. Every step below happens under the same roof, directed by the person who drew it.

  1. Phase 01

    Drawings

    The plans start in this office. Jamie draws the house, so there is no architect to translate and no plan to inherit.

  2. Phase 02

    Permits and engineering

    City permit procurement and engineering planning are handled in house, on the same set of drawings.

  3. Phase 03

    Construction

    Jamie personally directs every trade on site, from the footings through to the finishing landscapers.

  4. Phase 04

    Interiors

    Interior design closes the job under the same roof that drew it, so nothing gets handed off at the end.

Jamie Vaughn.

Designing, building and remodelling in Phoenix, Scottsdale, Paradise Valley and Arcadia since 1986.

Jamie Vaughn, owner

Jamie has been designing, building and remodelling luxury homes since 1986, after starting in the trade in the 1970s. He directs every project himself, from the first sketch to the finishing landscapers.

His subcontractors are trained well enough that each of them supervises on site. He says the office takes the jobs others turn down, including build-to-suit houses on difficult mountainside lots.

Jamie’s words and every claim in this section come from Carmel’s own About page.

05  How it works

No handoffs.

What changes when the person who drew the house is the person building it.

  • One office, start to finishBlueprints, city permits, engineering, construction and interior design under one roof, so there are no handoffs between firms and no one to point at when something slips.
  • The owner runs the jobJamie supervises personally rather than assigning a project manager. One team stays on the house from the first meeting to the handover of keys.
  • Designer and builder in oneBecause the person building the house also drew it, problems get resolved on site instead of going back to an architect for a revision.
  • Lower overhead, lower bidsNo showroom and no large back office. Jamie says that usually puts his number below competing bids on the same scope, by as much as 20 per cent.
