“I’m a designer/builder. That’s a big deal. Many home builders just go by someone else’s plan.”

Jamie Vaughn, owner, Carmel Homes Design Group

Jamie has been designing, building and remodelling luxury homes in Phoenix, Scottsdale, Paradise Valley and Arcadia since 1986, after starting in the trade in the 1970s. He directs every project himself, from the first sketch to the finishing landscapers.

His subcontractors are trained well enough that each of them supervises on site. He says the office takes the jobs others turn down, including build-to-suit houses on difficult mountainside lots.

Jamie’s words and every claim in this section come from Carmel’s own About page.

One office, start to finish

Blueprints, city permits, engineering, construction and interior design are handled under one roof, so there are no handoffs between firms and no one to point at when something slips.

The owner runs the job

Jamie supervises personally rather than assigning a project manager. One team stays on the house from the first meeting to the handover of keys.

Designer and builder in one

Because the person building the house also drew it, problems get resolved on site instead of going back to an architect for a revision.

Lower overhead, lower bids

No showroom and no large back office. Jamie says that usually puts his number below competing bids on the same scope, by as much as 20 per cent.
