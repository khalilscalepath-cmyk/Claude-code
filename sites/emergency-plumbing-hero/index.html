Shut the water off first

Main shutoff is usually by the street or near the water heater. Turn it clockwise. If you cannot reach it or it will not turn, call and we will talk you through it.

Water near anything electrical

If water is running near outlets or your breaker panel, get everyone out and call 911 first. Then call us. Nothing in a house is worth a shock.

Then call, do not wait

A slow leak overnight costs more than the callout. We would rather hear from you at 2am than see the damage at 9am.
